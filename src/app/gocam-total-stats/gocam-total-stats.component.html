Pathway count
{{stats.models}}
Curated S. pombe pathways. (Full list)
Total graph nodes
{{stats.nodes}}
The total count of nodes across all individual GO-CAM model graphs. The graph nodes are:
  • genes that enable activities
  • complexes that enable activities
  • target genes or complexes
  • chemicals
Total graph edges
{{stats.edges}}
The total count of edges across all individual GO-CAM model graphs. It includes all edges between graph nodes.
Connected activity nodes
{{stats.activities}}
Count of connected activity nodes (the core building block of a GO-CAM: a gene product or complex performing a specific molecular function) across all individual GO-CAM model graphs (excludes connections to chemicals and target genes that are not yet modelled as activities).
Chemicals
{{stats.chemicals}}
The total count of nodes across all individual GO-CAM model graphs representing chemicals.
Target genes
{{stats.target_genes}}
The total count of genes across all individual GO-CAM model graphs which are represented as targets of an activity, but are not yet modelled as enabling any activity.
Total GO term occurrences
{{stats.total_go_term_occurrences}}
The total count of all instances of GO terms used across all individual GO-CAM model graphs. Individual uses of the same GO term are counted separately.
Count of GO term used
{{stats.distinct_go_terms}}
The count of GO terms use across all GO-CAM pathways. Individual uses of the same GO term are not counted separately.
Raw nodes
{{stats.raw_nodes}}
The total count of all the nodes underlying the GO-CAM structure across all individual GO-CAM models. It includes every node that is included in the total graph nodes count, as well as every instance of a GO term and every supporting evidence and provenance for all annotations in the graph.
Raw edges
{{stats.raw_edges}}
The total count of edges between all the raw nodes across all individual GO-CAM models.
