### *Genes to Genomes*: "Science builds on science, but the databases making that possible are at risk"

<!-- newsfeed_thumbnail: pombase-logo-32x32px.png -->

[Jenny Montooth](https://genestogenomes.org/author/jmontooth/) has
[written in Genes to Genomes](https://genestogenomes.org/science-builds-on-science-but-the-databases-making-that-possible-are-at-risk/) about the
funding struggles of PomBase and other databases.

Article: [Science builds on science, but the databases making that possible are at risk](https://genestogenomes.org/science-builds-on-science-but-the-databases-making-that-possible-are-at-risk/)
