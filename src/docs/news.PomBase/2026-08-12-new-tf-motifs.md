### New addition: Transcription factor binding motifs JBrowse tracks on PomBase
<!-- pombase_flags: frontpage -->
<!-- newsfeed_thumbnail: pombase-logo-32x32px.png -->

Last year, we added more than 50 transcription factor binding motifs
from 
[Skribbe, Soneson, Stadler et al. (2025)](/reference/PMID:40015273)
to the
[DNA binding site consensus sequences page](/browse-curation/dna-binding-sites),
bringing the total number of consensus sequences listed to 74.

We have now updated our binding site consensus browser track for the
first time in about a decade. In this update, we have mapped the new
TF binding motifs from Skribbe et al. onto the genome by using the
PWMScan tool as described on [this page](/documentation/pombase-jbrowse-tracks).
Additionally, we have separated the original Consensus transcription
factor DNA binding motifs track to provide distinct tracks for each
motif. You can find individual links to these browser tracks in the
Genome browser column on the
[Consensus sequence page](/browse-curation/dna-binding-sites).
