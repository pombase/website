### GO-CAM progress: models now cover 1661 genes
<!-- pombase_flags: frontpage -->
<!-- newsfeed_thumbnail: pombase-logo-32x32px.png -->

Our GO-CAM models now cover [1661 protein-coding genes](/results/from/id/3a101c38-c4be-47e7-bfe0-52242f8cff9c)
representing over one third of the proteome and 38% of proteins with known biological processes.
The number of connected models is also increasing; this is a portion of our ["Summary map"](/gocam/summary/connected).
Visit the [PomBase GO-CAM pathways page](/gocam) to find out more.
