From the conference organisers:

---------------

We are very happy to announce that the website for the 13th
International Fission Yeasts Meeting is now online! 

[event.gayakoa.com/13thFissionYeastMeeting](https://event.gayakoa.com/13thFissionYeastMeeting)

On the website, you will find all you need to know about different
aspects of the meeting, including a provisional program (with more
details to come in the future – stay tuned!), registration, and
practical information about Bordeaux.

While we are still working on the details of the program, we wanted to
put the website online so that you can reserve the dates, plan your
trip, and benefit from the best fares for both travel and
accommodation. With respect to visas, please check the “Information”
page.

The meeting is now open for registration. Please note that
registration fees do not include accommodation, and you can find a
number of suggestions on the “Information” page.
