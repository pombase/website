### *Genetics*: "Invisible infrastructure at risk"
<!-- pombase_flags: frontpage -->
<!-- newsfeed_thumbnail: pombase-logo-32x32px.png -->

Are you concerned about funding for scientific knowledgebases? Our new
[opinion piece in *Genetics*](https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/iyag274)
(Knowledgebase and Database Resources issue) explains how the current
funding crisis threatens foundational database infrastructure, and
with it, basic science. We use three UK knowledgebases (including
PomBase) as case studies.

[Invisible infrastructure at risk: funding crises threaten global bioscience research](https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/iyag274)
