### Viewer for Complex Portal data
<!-- pombase_flags: frontpage -->
<!-- newsfeed_thumbnail: pombase-logo-32x32px.png -->

We have added the Complex Portal’s data and ComplexViewer (developed by the
[the Rappsilber lab](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/33/22/3673/4061280))
to the PomBase gene pages. ComplexViewer provides 2D diagrams of
macromolecular complex topology, showing sequence features relevant to
the interaction.

[![Complex Viewer example](assets/newsfeed/sfr1-complex-viewer-small.png){ .screenshot loading="lazy" width=100% }](assets/newsfeed/sfr1-complex-viewer-large.png)

Access this view with the “Protein complex” menu item on the left side of gene
pages. If a gene product belongs to more than one complex, a pull-down menu
lists all.

Example from the [sfr1 gene page](/gene/SPBC28F2.07).
