### Viewer for Complex Portal data
<!-- pombase_flags: frontpage -->
<!-- newsfeed_thumbnail: pombase-logo-32x32px.png -->


We have added the Complex Portal’s ComplexViewer (developed by
[the Rappsilber lab](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/33/22/3673/4061280)),
and data to the PomBase gene pages.  Access this view with the
"Protein complex" menu item on the left side of gene pages.

ComplexViewer provides 2D diagrams of macromolecular complex topology, showing
sequence features relevant to the interaction, such as binding domains, along
with stoichiometry information.

If a gene product belongs to more than one complex, a pull-down menu lists
all.
